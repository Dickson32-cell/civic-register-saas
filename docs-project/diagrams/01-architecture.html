Multi-Tenant Civic Register Platform

One product, many institutions. Each district, assembly or institution gets its own branded register system and its own isolated database — served by a single codebase you control.

Pricing

GHS 4,000 per institution per year (≈US$345 at ~11.6). Flat for every client — the more institutions you hold, the more you earn.

Tenancy

One Next.js app on Vercel; one isolated Neon database created per institution at signup; Postgres RLS seals each tenant's data.

Customisation

Logo, colours, name and subdomain per tenant, plus KoBoCollect-style custom fields their officers can add and rename.

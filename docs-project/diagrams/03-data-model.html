Per-Tenant Data Model

Seven entities carry the whole product: who the customer is, what they collect, what their officers define, and what they pay. # = primary key, → = foreign key.

Control plane

Tenant, Subscription and Payment tables live in the platform database — this is your customer list and revenue ledger.

Tenant plane

User, FormDef, CustomField and Entry are created fresh inside each tenant's own isolated Neon database at signup.

Custom fields

entry.data is JSONB — each tenant's officers define fields KoBoCollect-style; the register, tables and bills render from it.
